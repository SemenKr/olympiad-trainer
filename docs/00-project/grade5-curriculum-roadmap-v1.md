## Recommendation and scope

Build a coherent, supervised Grade 5 **training** program before claiming comprehensive VSOSh preparation. Start by validating an entry route using existing material, then fill demonstrated representation and reasoning gaps. Preserve official year/stage/region/platform differences and the distinction between problem demand and observed learner performance. Current numeric/set answers cannot establish independent proof competence or reproduce all official formats.

The 60-problem catalog has substantial reasoning content, including complete enumeration, bounds and witnesses, constraints and guarantees. It is not yet evidence of a novice-ready curriculum. Packs are existing three-problem access units, not validated lesson order or prerequisite levels. Grade and official task number must not become training difficulty. Proposed sequences below are research selections; they do not replace current Practice, Learning Path, Adaptive Practice or Simulation contracts.

The [project vision](project-vision.md) establishes Grade 5 mathematics/VSOSh-first scope and independent thinking. The specific batch sizes, instructional envelope and release gates below are **our recommendations**, not official requirements or already adopted product decisions. The official 2026/27 methodology supports varied, justified, constructive reasoning and appropriate presentation; it supplies no full Grade 5 syllabus or catalog-size target.

## Proposed training envelope to adopt or revise

Use the existing provisional capabilities as analytical lenses, with concepts separate. A bounded initial envelope should make room for:

1. Understanding and representing quantities/conditions; logical negation; checking a result against its meaning.
2. Constraint coordination and local exclusion; finite search with an explicit completeness argument.
3. Constructing and checking a witness; distinguishing one example from a general conclusion.
4. Bounds, attainment/optimality and universal guarantees; explaining why a claimed impossibility covers all cases.
5. Applying these obligations across the existing quantities, number-properties, geometry/measurement, order/structure, logical-relations and counting/selection families, with visual as well as verbal representations.

This is an interpretation of existing research, not a new taxonomy or a claim that every family must combine with every capability. Deferred geometry/cycle/common-multiple candidates stay deferred. A Grade 5 educator should check prerequisites, language and age suitability; learner observations should test the proposed sequence. Identify exclusions explicitly, especially unsupported proof/construction response modes and any source-specific competition formats.

## When another subject is justified

Recommend expansion only after the Grade 5 mathematics v1 scope is adopted and its gates are met, or after an explicit owner decision to pause/limit that program with known consequences. The evidence should show a usable first session and repeated supervised use, a maintained coherent content route, resolved release-blocking math/rights/representation issues, and an affordable human review process. A named specialist and verified source/rights plan for the new subject are prerequisites, with capacity to maintain mathematics after expansion.

A small separately approved discovery study may precede a new subject's implementation. Do not infer taxonomy transfer across subjects, create a cross-subject schema now, or use reaching 120 problems as the trigger. The current priority is validating the Grade 5 route, not broadening the product surface.
