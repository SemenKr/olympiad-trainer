## Internal duplication review

Astra inspected A–R catalog statements and the separate S/T modules before design; implementation retains that bounded comparison. U1 uses overlapping pair totals and double counting, unlike T's proportional digit reconstruction. Related general skills occur in «Три загадочных числа» and «Числа на гранях куба», but neither uses this pairwise reconstruction with verification of all conditions. U2 counts unordered subsets by their smallest card, unlike T's ordered colour-multiset strings, existing coin counts/digit mountains, and «Возраст братьев» with changing products and one surviving answer. U3 optimizes deterministic production with two resource constraints, unlike T's random-draw guarantee, seating coincidence, or geometric extremal arrangements. Similar broad reasoning goals are intentional; the tasks are not intended as numerical reskins. This is an internal comparison, not an exhaustive outside plagiarism search.
