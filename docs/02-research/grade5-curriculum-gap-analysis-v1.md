## Evidence basis and limits

The audit covers all 60 definitions, all 18 ordered Pack tuples, provenance records, assessments, 180 hints, 60 solutions and five authored reasoning checkpoints. It reuses the [source map](vsosh-source-map.md), [initial content audit](problem-content-audit.md), [candidate inventory](candidate-skill-inventory.md), [taxonomy validation](skill-taxonomy-validation.md), [provisional taxonomy](skill-taxonomy-v0.1.md), and the [J–L](content-scale-v2-batch-2.md), [M–O](content-expansion-batch-1.md) and [P–R](content-expansion-batch-2.md) dossiers. The current catalog is 60/18; the project vision's older 42/12 inventory is historical and must not be used as the current denominator.

A separate read-only researcher checked current primary-source methodology and concrete organiser publications. Coverage interpretation was then made against the authored solution paths, not inferred from task titles, source numbering or answer correctness. The six-family table is a descriptive single-primary-family assignment, not an official distribution. A later independent documentation review checks the synthesis; neither agent review nor passing software tests replaces a human mathematical/content-rights approval.

This is not a representative national sample, a comparison against a complete official topic syllabus, a revalidation of every historical source PDF, or a learner-outcome study. All catalog provenance is Moscow Grade 5; regional comparison below broadens the evidence but does not remove that selection bias. Current deployment/operational safety is outside this documentation task and must be checked separately for a pilot.

## Meaningful coverage gaps, not invented syllabus requirements

The [taxonomy](skill-taxonomy-v0.1.md) already distinguishes foundation modelling/negation, constraint coordination, local exclusion, complete enumeration, construction, bounds, optimality, guarantees and global impossibility. The catalog contains authored examples of each; this is **presence**, not balanced depth or a teachable route. The greatest gap is an independently validated transition between these obligations.

- **Represent and check before calculating:** balance and constant-error stories are plausible low-load candidates, but quantity naming, units, calendar conventions, fractions, signed intermediate contributions and equation notation remain assumed in several solutions. `diamond-emerald-balance` → `constant-scale-error` is a candidate contrast, not demonstrated transfer.
- **Enumerate versus explain completeness:** `six-position-colorings`, `exact-coin-payments` and `pages-without-digit-one` offer different counting structures. The first allows small explicit enumeration; later cases need non-overlap and coverage. More cosmetic number changes would provide repetition but not evidence of strategic transfer.
- **One rejection versus impossibility:** `granddaughters-first` and `bicycle-lock-code` support local constraint work; `table-impossible-sums` and grid-path optimality require an argument ruling out a whole class. The learner-facing bridge has not been validated.
- **A bound versus optimality:** `nonadjacent-row-seating`, `eighteen-piece-pie-cuts` and `liar-council-maximum` include attaining witnesses. The correct maximum alone cannot show the learner knows why the witness and bound are both necessary. Guarantee tasks also require a worst failed case, not merely one favorable example.
- **Visual measurement/construction:** interpreting a figure, distinguishing perimeter from area, checking a geometric construction and explaining a rearrangement remain thin or absent as observable activities. Existing deferred geometry candidates are research leads, not newly adopted skills.
- **Number properties:** six primary number-property rows mostly concern multiples, digits, factors and borrowing. Parity appears elsewhere but is not a validated sequence for impossibility; primality is not meaningfully developed. Broader divisibility/remainder/number-structure candidates warrant source sampling, not automatic syllabus inclusion.
- **Strategy choice across stories:** double counting appears in flights, attendance, cube faces and grid neighbors, yet is not a normalized v0.1 method. These are useful contrast candidates for existing modelling/counting capabilities; this document does not extend the taxonomy. Similarly, recursive reduction, invariant-like reasoning and adversarial strategy lack a validated novice introduction.

## Novice readiness and validation design

The [Foundations prototype](olympiad-foundations-prototype-v0.1.md) records one owner-reported novice experience: navigation was understandable but mathematical effort high and hints sometimes insufficient. Exact build, tasks, wording and raw learner work are unavailable. This is a hypothesis-generating account, not a recurring measured result. The [learner cards](olympiad-foundations-learner-cards-v0.1.md) provide an optional worked example and a near-reuse probe; card B is explicitly **not an independent transfer test**.

| Learning opportunity   | Current evidence                                                                                        | Next observable question                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Introductory reasoning | Selected simpler-looking catalog tasks and research cards; no validated entry sequence.                 | Can the learner explain the condition and choose a first representation without an adult supplying the strategy?                                |
| Worked example         | Every task has a revealable solution; cards have an optional demonstration.                             | Does the child understand why the step is valid, rather than copy notation? Record whether demonstration was requested.                         |
| Independent practice   | Existing Practice permits attempts/retries; catalog alone cannot establish independent strategy choice. | What was independently attempted before hints, and which support changed the next action?                                                       |
| Repetition             | Related families exist across Packs; no evidence that Pack order creates a planned spaced sequence.     | Can the learner return on another day with the support level documented, without adult cueing?                                                  |
| Transfer               | Several structural contrasts above are candidates. No empirical transfer evidence established.          | Does the learner select and justify the method in a changed context where the method is not announced? Compare with near repetition separately. |

Use [Learner Validation v0](learner-validation-v0.md), its [observation template](learner-validation-observation-template.md) and [LV-03 field sheet](learner-validation-lv03-field-sheet.md): staged first learner then at least three distinct learners for the research track, first visits around 20–40 minutes, separate return and Simulation observations before closing that track. Retain guardian consent/child assent, pseudonymous written observations and voluntary stopping. Do not force errors, hints, eligibility or completion; a recurring issue needs independent observations as the protocol specifies. This sample supports usability/problem discovery, not efficacy or mastery calibration.

Optional additional curriculum observation, proposed only: use an existing paper card or existing low-load problem, record the child's first representation, offer the existing support only as needed, then use a changed-story task on a return visit. Keep raw observation, assistance and interpretation separate under [Learning Evidence v0.1](learning-evidence-v0.1.md). No automatic scoring, new telemetry or claims of mastery are authorized.

## Human mathematical and source-rights gates

For **every** selected problem, a named qualified human should review the exact authored revision against the cited original and official key: preserved assumptions, changed quantities/format, all valid answers, existence/uniqueness, hint validity and full solution. Where the official file supplies only an answer, an independently worked derivation is required; it must not be called an official solution. Review must include visual meaning when a source diagram was removed. This is a proposed release record requirement, not a claim that the existing pipeline already stores such sign-offs.

Prioritize these review burdens before wider learner use:

| Existing examples                                                                   | Specific verification obligation                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `boastful-fisherman-streak`, `untouched-matchstick-figures`                         | Check every inequality/window and both extremal directions; prove strategy survives all legal opponent responses, not only a cooperative line. |
| `central-coin-column`, `maximum-grid-neighbor-sum`, `table-impossible-sums`         | Verify incidence multiplicities, global impossibility, witness legality, all table/path constraints and attaining totals.                      |
| `last-student-friends`, `rabbit-middle-cage`                                        | Check recursive reduction/existence or all neighbor counts, mutuality, endpoints and nonempty assumptions.                                     |
| `four-houses-distance-cases`, `crow-wire-positions`, `three-numbers-digit-sums`     | Ensure the case partition is exhaustive and wording preserves distinctness, order and digit-membership assumptions.                            |
| `cutout-area-ratio`, cube tasks, `circular-table-seat-count`, Packs P–R adaptations | Compare textual substitutes with original visual/physical constraints; verify no unintended alternative interpretation.                        |
| `neighbor-comparison-codes`, `apple-harvest-assignments`, all set responses         | Check every option, all witnesses, completeness, uniqueness where claimed, and whether adaptation provides cues absent in source.              |

This prioritization is not a report of wrong answers. Software tests can check a finite enumeration or witness where useful, but cannot confer source fidelity, general proof correctness or pedagogical suitability. No new correctness tests were written merely to mirror existing answers.

Public archive access is **not** verified permission to reproduce/adapt/distribute statements, figures or solutions. Rights review must establish applicable terms/permission for the intended pilot and publication scope, attribution and adaptation conditions, plus any exclusions. Where permission is unresolved, use a cleared item, an independently authored and reviewed alternative, or a reference-only research activity; do not silently copy a source or call the catalog rights-cleared. New original content still needs mathematical and pedagogical review. These are unresolved gates, not legal conclusions.
