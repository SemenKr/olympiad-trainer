## Training organization and review

- P, “Считаем части”: paper triangles, clay pieces, border tiles.
- Q, “Сравниваем условия”: gemstone balance, scale error, rabbit cages.
- R, “Порядок и изменения”: reversed-number tens, painted corridor, queue exchanges.

This order and these names are our training organization, not official taxonomy, difficulty or a prerequisite sequence. Each task has newly authored focus, strategy and next-step hints, followed by a separate protected solution. Candidate mathematics review preceded implementation; final code/content review and QA must inspect the actual authored revision. Automated validation establishes structural and identity contracts, not mathematical truth.

## Human release gates and reuse rights

Official publication establishes provenance and access, not an adaptation license. The [official publication notice](https://vos.olimpiada.ru/news/1784) describes public availability; targeted research found no affirmative reuse license. Newly authored concise adaptations, hints and explanations do not themselves settle rights in the underlying material. This dossier records references and analysis rather than reproducing full source statements or solutions. No legal permission is claimed.

Before public release, a human must verify each row against the linked originals and official answers, approve mathematical correctness and the listed clarifications, and establish a permitted basis for reuse/adaptation. If that basis cannot be established, keep the content unreleased and replace or remove affected tasks in a separately authorized change. A Draft PR supports that review; it does not satisfy these gates. Merge, deployment, Production migration and Production data changes remain outside this task's authority.
