# Novice Entry & Return v1 — product and learning design

**Status: READY for human decision; not adopted and not cleared for learner use.** Research/design only, 18 October 2026. Fresh origin/main baseline `7aa3435332a83d24328626b40fbf07efe02bc227`. No sessions were run, no content approvals obtained, and no application or learning semantics changed.

## Recommendation and authority

Start with a parent-facilitated selection of existing Pack prefixes, not a new onboarding feature. Offer two or three problems in a first **20–30 minute** visit, and at most two changed-context problems in a separate return visit. These are scheduling budgets, never speed or completion targets. Stopping early is valid. Select **eight candidates**, not an eight-task assignment. Use existing Practice attempts, optional hints, retry, early Finish and factual Summary. Observe whether a child can choose a meaningful next action; do not judge mastery, weakness, giftedness or instructional effectiveness.

The [Grade 5 roadmap](../00-project/grade5-curriculum-roadmap-v1.md) is a proposal even though merged. Its first slice motivates this design but does not authorize adoption. The adopted [Learner Validation v0](learner-validation-v0.md), [Learning Path](../04-architecture/learning-path-v1.md), [identity/recovery](../04-architecture/learner-identity-recovery-v1.md), [Practice Journey](../04-architecture/practice-journey-v1.md) and [Finish UX](../03-ux/finish-encouragement-v0.md) govern behavior. The [Foundations prototype](olympiad-foundations-prototype-v0.1.md) and [learner cards](olympiad-foundations-learner-cards-v0.1.md) remain optional research material with their own human approval gate.

The [all-60 matrix](grade5-curriculum-coverage-v1.md) supplies per-ID source URLs/pages and code evidence; the [gap analysis](grade5-curriculum-gap-analysis-v1.md) explains limitations. Application source is unchanged between that matrix's baseline and this baseline. Candidate selection and sequence below are pedagogical hypotheses using the existing [taxonomy](skill-taxonomy-v0.1.md), not official difficulty or a new curriculum standard. The one owner-reported novice experience in Foundations lacks exact-build/raw-work evidence; it does not establish a recurring problem.

## Access and real progress consequences

Use the existing chooser's freely selectable Packs. Internal route evidence: [Pack page](../../src/app/practice/pack/page.tsx) and [registered tuples](../../src/modules/practice/application/completed-practice-episode.ts). Pack links use `/practice/pack?pack=pack-a` (likewise d, j, m, o, q); no per-problem deep link is proposed.

- A1, M1, D1, Q1 and J1 are first items. Finish voluntarily after the selected first item; do not force Skip on later items to exit.
- O2 requires O1 first through ordinary navigation. Q2 requires Q1. Do not bypass a predecessor or fabricate a solved/skipped state. If prerequisite or navigation is blocking, defer the second item; do not let a parent answer just to unlock it.
- A, M, D and J have other tasks; O3 and Q3 are also outside this selection. Do not instruct continuation into unreviewed material. For this scoped study, continue beyond the selected prefix only after a separate approval/replan; otherwise offer early Finish or pause. If the learner wants an unrelated task, record the deviation and defer this planned activity rather than coercing completion.
- An unfinished draft takes precedence. Let the child resume it or use the existing explicit Finish flow by choice before selecting another Pack. Never discard/reset work to preserve the script. Pausing keeps the unfinished episode; Finish closes it. Reopening a finished Pack starts its normal sequence, not a continuation at the next unused problem.
- A successfully persisted early Pack Finish creates the existing Path marker, potentially even with an incorrect/unfinished attempt or solution exposure. It is **not** “all three solved”. Thus this design can mark several Packs after prefixes; explain that consequence to the parent before consent to the route. Do not backfill, clear or suppress markers, XP, history or adaptive side effects.
- Home retains Resume → adaptive → fresh core → guided Pack precedence. Observe the natural first action first. A parent-proposed move to the chosen Pack is a **prompted study navigation**, not spontaneous discovery or an override of Home. Summary's guided next Pack need not be the planned next item; use existing free choice with permission to stop. If the learner prefers natural Home/Path, record that route and defer the planned selection.

This is a facilitated learning observation embedded in ordinary Practice, not a replacement for an uncoached Home usability test. Choice of study tasks contaminates conclusions about independent task selection, but need not erase independently chosen mathematical actions once on the task.

Before the visit, the owner/educator clears the exact selected content and route, and the guardian confirms consent and child assent. Use the family's normal device/browser profile for this learner; record actual build, not just this design baseline. Guardian may arrange existing optional recovery privately outside the mathematics budget; code/cookie/token never enter notes. One browser profile represents one learner. Do not create or reset an identity for an observation.

Do not present this route as easier or assign it because the learner made an error. It is a choice of context; the same observation and stopping rules apply.

## Separate return: continuity first, changed context second

Propose a different day, approximately 2–7 days later for family convenience; record the actual interval and outside practice. This is a scheduling hypothesis, not a spacing prescription or retention test. Budget 15–25 minutes, stop earlier freely. Use the same learner/browser profile and normal identity. Do not clear cookies, use Start Fresh, delete history or trigger recovery to manufacture a return. If access fails naturally, follow the adopted recovery UI with the guardian privately; stop dependent learning if continuity cannot be established. Recovery restores identity/history, not a promise to import another device's local draft.

1. **First 3–5 minutes:** observe what the child expects and chooses on Home. If unfinished Practice exists, resume it first. This is same-task continuation, not a fresh transfer probe. Do not force its Finish merely to reach planned tasks. If it uses the visit, defer changed-context work.
2. **Next 6–10 minutes:** after A1 on the first visit, offer M1 without saying “use the previous method”. Record study navigation as prompted. Ask about checking only after the child's attempt, without requiring a particular representation. If M1 was already seen elsewhere, classify exposure and do not call it novel.
3. **Optional further 5–7 minutes:** offer D1 only if O2 was actually attempted previously, the new prerequisites are suitable and the child wants more. Otherwise omit it; a fresh D1 attempt may be useful later but is not evidence of transfer from an unattempted O2. Finish M through the existing flow before D. On the quantity route, use J1 instead of the M/D comparison, subject to price/division prerequisites.
4. **Last 2–3 minutes:** Finish or pause by choice; record expected versus observed continuity and one neutral reflection. Do not coach a proof to achieve an acceptance target. Never ask the child to complete every candidate across the two visits.

Repeating Q1 to reach Q2 is exact repetition; Q2 then is a same-context contrast. It is not a substitute for the requested changed-context M1/D1/J1 probes. An actual return may end with only continuity observed; report that honestly and arrange another visit only if a consequential question remains and the family agrees.

## Observation categories and testable learner criteria

Use two separate dimensions: **intervention** and **relationship to prior exposure**. These are written research annotations, not app events, scores or learner states.

- Independent mathematical action: the child selects and attempts a relevant action before a method cue on that task. Record previous similar tasks/examples; “unassisted now” does not mean independently discovered or transferred.
- Prompted action/explanation: follows a neutral script question, navigation invitation or suggestion to use available support. Preserve the exact prompt; distinguish UI prompt from mathematical cue. A method cue or adult demonstration is assisted reasoning, not neutral prompting.
- App-supported work: record focus/strategy/next-step/full solution and when it was seen, separately from adult support. An independently clicked strategy hint still supplies a strategy.
- Exact repetition/continuation: same task or resumed draft. Near repetition: close reuse such as Foundations B after A, with prior demonstration exposure recorded. Potential transfer: changed-context work such as A1→M1 or O2→D1 without announcing the method, with prerequisites and prior exposure recorded. It remains a hypothesis; one successful answer or explanation does not establish transfer.

For each criterion record **observed unassisted / observed after prompt or support / not observed / blocked**, plus action/words and exposure. These are acceptance observations about the experience, not a child pass/fail test. No percentage, response-time cutoff or giftedness inference.

Follow LV v0 staging: first learner to test the protocol, then its broader distinct-learner plan if continued. This two-visit design does not close LV v0; separate Simulation remains outside these visits and required separately for that track's exit. Apply its Critical stop and recurring-issue rule, including two independent sessions for recurring meaningful usability evidence; repeated trials of one task are not automatically independent evidence.

## Smallest next slice and decisions

**Recommend no application implementation yet.** The smallest useful next slice is human clearance of the default prefix set plus one supervised first/return observation using the [parent protocol](novice-entry-return-parent-protocol-v1.md), existing Practice and optional paper Foundations. Zero new problems, routes, Pack tuples, schema, scoring, mastery fields or telemetry. Do not change Home/adaptive priority or call this an automatically assigned beginner path. Record actual history/Path consequences normally.

If observation demonstrates a recurring consequential barrier, propose only the smallest evidenced correction: existing chooser/Finish wording or hierarchy if navigation blocks; an existing prerequisite explanation if that specific gap blocks a sensible plan; a separately adopted optional Foundations entry/exit proposal only if its distinct hypothesis is supported. Current evidence does **not** select one of these implementations. Any feature needs explicit adoption of scope, content clearance, accessible/recoverable delivery and exposure interpretation before coding. A new lesson engine or per-problem routing is not justified by this design.

Open decisions: owner/educator accepts a route and prefix-Finish consequences; named reviewer clears exact items and rights; guardian/child choose participation and optional example; owner later decides whether observations justify an implementation. Recommended defaults are the A/O → M/D route, skipping unready/unapproved comparisons, and no app change. These decisions block learner use or implementation, not completion of this research artifact. Status is READY for those decisions, not a launch approval.
