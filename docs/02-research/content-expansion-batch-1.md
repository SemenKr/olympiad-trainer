# Content Expansion Batch 1 — source and math dossier

## Scope and review status

This batch adds nine Grade 5 mathematics tasks from official Moscow VSOSh source archives as three ordinary Practice Packs. The current catalog’s 42 problems and 12 Packs were checked before selection. All nine exact source task references differ from the catalog. Candidate adaptations were independently reviewed for answer, reasoning, edge cases, supported answer shape and Grade 5 explanation fit. This review is evidence for human review; it does not replace human approval of mathematical content, adaptation accuracy or reuse rights.

Page numbers below are PDF page numbers, counted from the first page. `I` is the original task source; `IS` is the official answer/solution source. Source facts are distinct from our training adaptation and our pedagogical ordering.

## Packs and interpretation

The ordered Pack tuples are:

- Pack M, “Условия и выводы”: `false-box-labels`, `pirate-coin-redistribution`, `bicycle-lock-code`.
- Pack N, “Позиции и подсчёт”: `crow-wire-positions`, `maximum-grid-neighbor-sum`, `rectangle-knight-count`.
- Pack O, “Числа и закономерности”: `winter-day-number`, `six-position-colorings`, `five-lamp-pattern`.

The names and order are product-authored training organization, not official source taxonomy or difficulty. Each problem has independently authored focus, strategy and next-step hints plus a protected training solution. Official statements, hints, difficulty and pedagogical interpretations are not represented as such.

## Rights and human verification

The official PDFs support provenance and answer verification; they do not grant permission to reproduce task statements, diagrams, solutions or other protected material. The app uses concise adaptations and newly authored hints and explanations. A human must review whether the adaptations remain faithful and whether this use is permitted before the content is treated as approved for publication. No full source statement or artwork is stored in this dossier.
