## Minimum supervised pilot

Use [Learner Validation v0](../02-research/learner-validation-v0.md), the existing [observation template](../02-research/learner-validation-observation-template.md) and [LV-03 field sheet](../02-research/learner-validation-lv03-field-sheet.md). Record the exact tested build, device/input and S/T beta restriction. Mark unavailable Simulation, Review, adaptive and Learning Path scenarios as not applicable. Keep identifiable/raw notes outside the repository under the existing privacy rules.

1. Confirm guardian permission and child assent; say “Мы проверяем приложение, а не тебя…” and that stopping is allowed. Prepare a verified isolated learner context, paper and pencil. Do not explain the buttons first.
2. On Home ask “У тебя есть немного времени позаниматься олимпиадной математикой. Покажи, что бы ты сделал.” Record the spontaneous first action, choice of Start or chooser, any expectation about S/T order, and each adult intervention.
3. Let the learner attempt independently. Do not force an error, hint, solution or Skip. Observe check/retry and help discovery. If a hint is naturally opened, ask once “Что ты теперь попробуешь сделать?” Record the actual next action and exact assistance; time spent thinking alone is not a usability defect.
4. At a natural pause ask how they would leave and return, and what they expect to remain. Observe Save/Exit → Home Continue and confirm answer, hints and position against that expectation. Refresh only with the learner's agreement; do not inject engineering faults during the session.
5. When the learner chooses to stop, observe Finish and ask “Расскажи своими словами, что эта страница тебе сейчас показывает.” Then inspect History/Progress and ask what it says about their work. Ask about XP only after the spontaneous explanation: did finishing, viewing a solution or skipping sound like independently solving or a knowledge score?
6. Return Home and observe the intended next action. Ask what was unclear and whether they would know where to return tomorrow. Separate observed facts, learner words, prompts/interventions, interpretations and alternative explanations in the existing template. Stop for fatigue or a Critical finding; follow the protocol's severity/recurrence rules.

The [Foundations prototype](../02-research/olympiad-foundations-prototype-v0.1.md) records one owner-reported learner for whom some hints were not useful; exact exposure/build/raw evidence was unavailable. This motivates another observation, not a diagnosis or teaching requirement. Distinguish unclear wording, inability to choose a next action, a specific prerequisite, competing viable reasoning and productive struggle. Do not interrupt Practice to teach the prototype. Any separate optional Foundations activity requires its existing human content/mathematics check and exposure/assent rules; it is not part of this minimum beta session.

Recommended next product action: conduct one supervised novice S/T session with these existing forms, prioritising the action taken after a naturally chosen hint and the learner's own explanation of Summary. Use that evidence before proposing instructional changes.
